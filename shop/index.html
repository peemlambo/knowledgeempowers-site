Questions

Before you buy

How does my child get access?

Message us on WhatsApp and we send the payment details. Once you have paid, your child's personal code arrives on WhatsApp, usually the same day.

How long does access last?

Online workbooks: 12 months from the day you pay, for every topic in the series. Exam packs: one school term.

Which grade should I buy?

Workbooks are made by topic, not by grade, so one book takes a learner from the first idea to the last. Not sure where to start? The free maths check shows which topics to work on first.

Does it work with my child's school?

Yes. It works alongside any curriculum: public schools, IEB and Cambridge.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone, tablet or computer with a browser. Each code works on up to two devices.

Do you have prices for more than one child?

A second child in the same family pays half price. Tutors and schools pay R79 per learner for 10 learners or more.

How are printed books delivered?

We print in full colour, bind them and post them to you. Delivery is charged separately.
