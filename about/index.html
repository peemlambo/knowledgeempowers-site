About

Who we are

KnowledgeEmpowers is home to GROW. Our maths workbooks and tutoring help learners catch up, keep up and get ahead with real understanding, not just a completed page. One topic, in depth. Works alongside any curriculum.

Who's behind GROW

GROW is run by Pelocus Org (Pty) Ltd, a registered South African company, not a one-person side project. That matters for a few concrete reasons:

  • Every tutor is vetted before they work with a child. Our vetting includes police clearance checks and reference checks: the same standard we'd want for our own children.
  • One team, one standard. The workbooks, the tutoring and the support you get when you email us all come from the same small team, so nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Your purchase is handled properly. When you buy through KnowledgeEmpowers, you're buying directly from Pelocus Org (Pty) Ltd (Reg. No. 2020/864381/07), trading as GROW: an accountable company, not an anonymous storefront.
