Privacy Policy

At KnowledgeEmpowers, we value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you visit our site, purchase digital products, or interact with affiliate links.

1. Child safety

Every tutor in the KnowledgeEmpowers network completes a Police Clearance Certificate and signs a child safeguarding declaration before they are ever matched with a learner. You're not just choosing a tutor: you're choosing someone who will spend time with your child, and we treat that responsibility accordingly. If you ever have a question or concern about a tutor's conduct, you're welcome to reach out to us directly at any time.

Every tutor is police-cleared before working with learners. Every tutor signs a child safeguarding declaration. Your information is handled securely and confidentially, in line with South Africa's POPIA.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Name and email address (when you contact us or make a purchase)
  • Payment details (processed securely via third-party platforms)
  • Usage data (via cookies and analytics tools)

3. How we use your information

We use your data to:

  • Process orders and deliver downloads
  • Respond to inquiries and provide support
  • Improve site performance and user experience
  • Share relevant affiliate offers (only those we trust)

4. Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you purchase through them, we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. We only promote tools and platforms we believe in.

5. Data protection

We take reasonable steps to protect your data:

  • Secure payment gateways
  • No selling or renting of personal information
  • Limited access to user data

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Request access to your data
  • Ask for corrections or deletion
  • Opt out of marketing communications

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact us

If you have questions, email us at knowledgeempowers08@gmail.com.
